MOVEMENT I · THE FIRST SPARK

By the Fireplace

Maison Margiela · REPLICA

By the Fireplace — Eau de Toilette

Some warmth lives in a room.

Some warmth follows you long after you leave it.

For the winter nights, and all the ones we have yet to spend together.

A memory of warmth, held close.

A Little Warmth

Pure Cashmere

100% cashmere shawl

MOVEMENT III · THE EVENING RITUAL

After the Day

Helena Rubinstein · Re-Plasty

Age Recovery Cream · Black Bandage

When the day has asked enough of you,

let the evening ask for nothing at all.

Only a little quiet, and a moment that belongs entirely to you.

A small gesture of care, at day’s end.

MOVEMENT IV · THE FINALE

Wherever You Go

Coach · Elodie

Black leather shoulder bag

For the keys, the little things, the everyday plans.

For the places we know, and the ones we have not found yet.

Take a piece of this winter with you, wherever you go.

For every beautiful ordinary day ahead.

For Flora,

May this winter be soft on you — warm rooms, quiet nights, and little moments that make everything feel lighter.

Even in the coldest season, I hope you always feel loved, cared for, and beautifully yourself.

Happy birthday, my love.
